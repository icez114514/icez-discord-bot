# Casino embossed deck

Exact copies of `casino_assets/embossed/cards/0.png` through `51.png` and
`casino_assets/embossed/back.png`. See `casino_assets/embossed/SOURCE.md` for
provenance. No font binaries are included. Index = suit * 13 + rank;
suits are spades/hearts/diamonds/clubs, ranks A through K.

Xr.png and Xb.png reuse stock(), centered_text() and jester() from the same
casino generator, with red/black JOKER lettering; no standard faces are changed.
